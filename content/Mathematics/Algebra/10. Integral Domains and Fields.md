# 10.1 Integral Domain and Fields
---
We first construct the following theorem to define the intergral domain.

<>

**Theorem 10.1.1** The following conditions are equivalent for a ring $R$. ^thm-10-1-1
1. If $ab=0$ in $R$, then either $a=0$ or $b=0$.
2. If $ab=ac$ in $R$ and $a\ne0$, then $b=c$.
3. If $ba=ca$ in $R$ and $a\ne0$, then $b=c$.
